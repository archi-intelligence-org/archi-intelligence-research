# archi-intelligence Research Series

A working-paper series on **architecture intelligence** — the maturity ladder of architectural reasoning, from drawing to autonomous design, anchored in automotive electrical/electronic (E/E) architecture and extending toward embodied AI.

All papers are released **Open Access under CC-BY 4.0**. This repository mirrors the freely available PDF editions; the authoritative, permanently archived versions live on Zenodo (DOIs below).

🌐 **Website:** https://www.archi-intelligence.org
📚 **Research index:** https://www.archi-intelligence.org/research/

## Papers

### 2026-01 · The Architectural Migration of the Century
*From Engineering Ontology to Embodied Intelligence*

A cross-industry survey tracing how E/E architecture converges at the infrastructure layer while control semantics and burden of proof diverge. Introduces two original frameworks: **AR0–AR5** (Architecture Readiness) and **AI²-ML** (Architecture Intelligence Maturity Levels).

- 📄 PDF: [Download](2026-01/2026-01_EN.pdf)
- 🔗 DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.20357858](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20357858)
- 🌐 Web: https://www.archi-intelligence.org/research/2026-01/

### 2026-02 · State of Automotive E/E Architecture 2026
*Measuring the Migration — 22 OEMs across the AR0–AR5 Ladder*

An empirical assessment of E/E architecture maturity across 22 major global automakers, scored on five dimensions and the AR0–AR5 ladder, in both deployed state and confirmed near-term roadmap.

- 📄 PDF: [Download](2026-02/2026-02_EN.pdf)
- 🔗 DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.20513865](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20513865)

### 2026-03 · Multi-Embodiment Physical AI Platform Readiness
*The Tesla FSD–Optimus Unified Stack*

The concluding paper: a clinical dissection of the Tesla FSD–Optimus unified stack from silicon to behavior, distilling eleven officially disclosed shared layers into three reuse mechanisms, and characterizing the precise boundary of cross-morphology reuse.

## Frameworks introduced

- **AR0–AR5 — Architecture Readiness**: a six-tier capability-threshold ladder (mechanical-electrical island → trusted general embodied agent), analogous in spirit to SAE J3016.
- **AI²-ML — Architecture Intelligence Maturity Levels**: a 5×5 maturity model for the tools/methodology dimension of architectural reasoning.

Both are released to the community under CC-BY 4.0 — citation, revision, and extension are welcome.
